Before you use it
  • Predicts any cognitive impairment (CDR ≥ 0.5) within 5 years, not biomarker-confirmed Alzheimer's disease. Applies only to people who are cognitively normal now.
  • Derived on 782 cognitively normal adults aged 42–97 in OASIS-3 (Knight ADRC, St. Louis), a mostly white, highly educated research cohort. Validation is internal only; it needs external testing, for example in ADNI.
  • Volumes must come from FreeSurfer or a comparable pipeline. Other software can measure on a different scale. In the cohort, total hippocampal volume was typically 5,580–9,520 mm³ (men 5,690–9,910, women 5,510–9,140; full range 4,010–10,870) and intracranial volume 1,160,000–1,840,000 mm³ (men 1,360,000–1,910,000, women 1,100,000–1,670,000; full range 870,000–2,030,000). Values outside the full range are extrapolations.
  • Recent depression may partly be an early symptom of decline rather than a cause. Treat it as a marker.
  • APOE "Unknown" scores 0 and underestimates risk for carriers, about one in three people in the cohort.
  • Hypertension carried no independent risk in this cohort (HR 1.05) and scores 0; a single yes/no history item may miss the effect of long-standing midlife blood pressure. Physical activity is not included: OASIS-3 does not record it.
Research prototype for discussion. Not a medical device and not for clinical decisions. Data: OASIS-3, Knight ADRC, Washington University in St. Louis.
